The challenge

A precision transmission components manufacturer relied almost entirely on a B2B marketplace for overseas leads. Their own English site existed but was slow, thin, and effectively invisible in search — buyers couldn't find it, and the ones who landed didn't trust the translated copy enough to send an inquiry.

The approach

One studio, three coordinated workstreams running in parallel:

Rebuild

A fast, crawlable site on HTML5 + Tailwind, restructured around product categories the way buyers search them — with specification tables and application content on every product page.

Rewrite

Every page rewritten in buyer-grade English for procurement engineers — clear specs, real use cases, and the trust signals that move a buyer from browsing to inquiring.

Rank

Technical SEO across all 1000+ pages, on-page optimization mapped to commercial keywords, and an E-E-A-T content layer that demonstrated genuine manufacturing expertise — exactly what Google rewards.

The results

Within six months, the site ranked on page 1 for over 100 commercial keywords and began producing qualified inquiries directly — buyers who arrived through search, not through a marketplace bid. The traffic kept compounding after launch, the way organic rankings do once they hold.

— Bob, TONGLU INC.
