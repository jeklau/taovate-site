The problem

Most exporters live on someone else's platform.

You pay to show up, you compete on price next to ten identical suppliers, and the moment you stop paying, the buyers vanish.

A foreign-trade independent site flips that. When a buyer searches for what you make and finds your site — fast, in clear English, with the proof they need — the inquiry comes to you directly. No bidding. No middleman. No rent.

The catch: most independent sites never get found. They're slow, written in translated English no buyer trusts, and invisible to Google. That's the gap we close.
